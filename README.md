
### AI-Powered Revenue Recovery & Customer Retention

Revora AI is a machine learning project designed to help businesses understand which customers may leave and how much revenue could be at risk. It uses customer and subscription data to predict churn and suggest practical ways to retain valuable customers.

### What it does

* Predicts customers who are likely to churn
* Identifies revenue that could be lost
* Groups customers based on their risk level
* Suggests suitable retention actions
* Shows insights through an interactive dashboard
* Helps businesses estimate potential revenue recovery



### How it works:-


Customer Data
      ↓
Data Cleaning & Analysis
      ↓
Machine Learning Model
      ↓
Churn Prediction
      ↓
Revenue at Risk
      ↓
Recovery Recommendation
      ↓
Dashboard


### Why we built it:-

The idea behind Revora AI is simple: **predicting churn is useful, but knowing which customers are worth saving is even more useful.**

The project connects machine learning predictions with actual business decisions, helping companies focus their retention efforts where they can make the most impact.
